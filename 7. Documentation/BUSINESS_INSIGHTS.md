# Business Insights Executive Summary



The Retail Merchandising Dashboard identifies revenue drivers, vendor performance gaps, and inventory risks.





---
## Revenue Insights



* Revenue performance remains stable with periodic fluctuation
* Category concentration exists
* Certain vendors contribute disproportionately to revenue





---
## Vendor Insights

Vendor scorecards reveal:

* Lead-time variation
* Revenue contribution imbalance
* Dependency risk exposure





---
## Inventory Insights

Inventory analysis indicates:

* Overstock risk in selected categories
* Potential stockout exposure
* Uneven inventory allocation





---
## Margin Insights

Revenue does not always align with profitability.

High-revenue vendors or products may not deliver strong margins.





---
## Strategic Recommendations

1. Reduce vendor concentration
2. Improve replenishment planning
3. Balance inventory allocation
4. Monitor margin and revenue together
5. Diversify merchandising portfolio





---
## Conclusion

The dashboard demonstrates strong merchandising visibility while identifying operational and strategic improvement opportunities.
